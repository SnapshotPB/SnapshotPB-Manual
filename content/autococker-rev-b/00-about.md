# About this manual

This manual describes the {{value product}} Autococker: the {{value board.name}} board, the firmware that runs on it, and the app that configures it. It tells you how to install the board, how to use the buttons and the screen, and what each setting does.

The manual is one file. It has no link to the internet, and it works from any folder. You can save it and read it with no connection.

The manual comes from two documents. The firmware document describes the firmware `{{value firmware.version}}`: its buttons, its screens, its settings, and how they work. The app document describes the app `{{value app.version}}` on each platform. A tool validates the two documents against a schema set and writes the tables of this manual. The chapter [Sources and versions](#sources-and-versions) lists each source and its version. This copy describes the board at `{{value board.md5}}`.

A setting has the same key in the firmware document and in the app document. Where both describe a setting, the manual shows a tab for the board and a tab for each platform of the app. Select a tab once, and every setting shows that platform.

The manual shows a warning, a caution or a note in this form:

WARNING: A warning tells you about a risk of injury. Read it before you do the step.

CAUTION: A caution tells you about a risk of damage to the board or to the marker.

NOTE: A note gives you information that helps you.
