# Bluetooth and the app

The Bluetooth radio is off at each start. Press MENU on the Bluetooth row of the Menu screen to turn the radio on. The screen shows `BT ON`, and the status LED goes blue. The board shows the name `Snapshot PB` to a phone.

The app reads and writes the settings of the board through Bluetooth. Some settings are in the app only:

- The name of each of the four profiles.
- The boot text, which is the text of the start screen.
- The display window, which is the part of the screen that the grip frame shows.

The Full window switch of the Setup screen shows the menu on the whole screen for this session. Use it when the display window is wrong and you cannot see the menu.
