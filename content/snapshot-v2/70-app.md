## Settings in the app only

Some settings are in the app only:

- The name of each of the four profiles.
- The boot text, which is the text of the start screen.
- The display window, which is the part of the screen that the grip frame shows.

The [Full window](#setting-runtime-display-window-full) switch of the Setup screen shows the menu on the whole screen for this session. Use it when the display window is wrong and you cannot see the menu.
