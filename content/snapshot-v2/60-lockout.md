# Lockout

Lockout is a lock on the configuration. It is not a lock on the firing. A locked board fires as a board that is not locked.

Press LOCKOUT to lock the board. The screen shows `LOCKED`, and the home screen shows the `LOCK` tag. A locked board does not keep a change from the menu, and it does not keep a change from the app. Press LOCKOUT again to unlock the board. The screen shows `UNLOCKED`.

The board keeps the lock through a power cycle. A locked board starts on the locked screen.
