# About this manual

This manual describes the {{value product}} board `{{value board.name}}` and the firmware that runs on it. It tells you how to install the board, how to use the buttons and the screen, and what each setting does.

The manual is one file. It has no link to the internet, and it works from any folder. You can save it and read it with no connection.

The manual comes from the sources of the board and of the firmware. A tool reads the menu tree, the protocol and the board record, and it writes the tables of this manual. The chapter [Sources and versions](#sources-and-versions) lists each source and its version. This copy describes the firmware `{{value firmware.version}}` and the board at `{{value board.md5}}`.

The manual shows a warning, a caution or a note in this form:

WARNING: A warning tells you about a risk of injury. Read it before you do the step.

CAUTION: A caution tells you about a risk of damage to the board or to the marker.

NOTE: A note gives you information that helps you.
